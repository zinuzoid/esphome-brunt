# Brunt service shutdown notice

Brunt app owners got this email from GoQual Inc. (the company operating Brunt) on 2026-06-30. The app
and cloud stopped working on 2026-08-01. The recipient's details and the mail headers have been removed.

* **From:** cx@goqual.com
* **Date:** Tue, 30 Jun 2026 17:05 +0900
* **Subject:** [Brunt] Brunt 앱 서비스 종료 및 제품 이용 안내

## English translation

> **Subject:** [Brunt] Brunt app service termination and product usage notice
>
> Hello, this is Brunt.
>
> We sincerely thank all customers who have used Brunt products and the app service.
> Following an internal operating decision, Brunt **will end the Brunt app service as of 1 August 2026**.
> We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience and confusion this sudden notice causes.
>
> The Brunt app service **remains available as usual until 31 July 2026**.
> After the service ends, app-based features will no longer be available, including launching the
> Brunt app, logging in, device registration, remote control and automation settings.
>
> However, this does not mean Brunt products themselves become entirely unusable after the shutdown.
> Features that need the app connection will be unavailable, but some functions can still be used
> according to each product's basic mode of operation.
>
> For example, products such as the Brunt Plug or Airjet will lose remote control, app control and
> automation after the shutdown, but can still be used like an ordinary power outlet or air purifier.
>
> For products still within the one-year free warranty period from the date of purchase, refunds can
> be requested once the purchase history is verified. Customers who want a refund should prepare
> proof of purchase and contact the **Shophlet Brunt chat support channel**
> (<https://m.smartstore.naver.com/shophlet>).
>
> ***Customers using Brunt products and services under a B2B contract will be contacted individually
> by a dedicated representative, according to their contract and usage.***
>
> After the shutdown, no support is available for app-based features such as app connection, device
> registration, device reset or remote control. Only enquiries about purchase-history verification
> and refunds for products within the warranty period will be handled.
>
> When the service ends, customers' personal information and service usage data will be retained or
> destroyed in accordance with the relevant laws and the privacy policy. Some information that must
> be kept for a certain period under the relevant laws will be kept for that period and then safely
> destroyed.
>
> No separate action is required from customers regarding the service termination.
>
> However, if you use remote control or automation through the app, please check how you will use
> your product before the termination date.
> Once again, we deeply thank all customers who trusted and used Brunt for so long.
> We will do our best to minimize any inconvenience to the very end.
>
> Thank you.
>
> Brunt service operator<br>
> GoQual Inc.
